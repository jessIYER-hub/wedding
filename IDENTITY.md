# Jessica & Gaurav · identity guide

For anyone making something for our wedding: signage, menus, stationery, screens, a cake topper, anything. Please use this rather than guessing.

**The idea, in one line:** we've always found our way back to each other. Warm, a little witty, never fussy.

## Colour

Three colours and one neutral. Nothing else.

| Name | Hex | Use | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ink navy | `#1B2433` | Text, lines, buttons | The navy from our engagement photos |
| Cream | `#F5EEE3` | Backgrounds, paper | Warm, like linen |
| Cocoa | `#6A4636` | Accent only: the "&", one highlighted word | The hot chocolate from our first date, at Dark Sugars |
| Stone | `#D9CFC1` | Hairlines, quiet fills | A neutral that sits between cream and navy |

Lighter cream for cards and inputs: `#FBF8F2`. Secondary text: `#4A5263`.

**Never:** gold foil or heavy gold, red, yellow, bright colours, gradients.

## Type

| Role | Typeface | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display (names, headings, big numbers) | **Fraunces**, Light (300) | High-contrast at large sizes. Free on Google Fonts. |
| Accent | **Fraunces Italic**, Light, in cocoa | Only for the "&" in our names and at most one word per piece. Never a whole sentence. |
| Body (everything else) | **Source Sans 3**, Regular and Semibold | Clear and calm. 18px minimum on screens; 10pt minimum in print. |

**No script or calligraphy fonts.** No all-caps headlines. Sentence case everywhere.

## The mark

- **Full:** Jessica *&* Gaurav (Fraunces Light, cocoa italic "&").
- **Short:** J *&* G (same treatment). Use it where space is tight: favicons, wax seals, napkins.
- Always our full first names: **Jessica**, not Jess, in the mark.
- Leave clear space around it equal to the height of the "J". Don't box it, outline it, add shadows, or set it in any other font.
- On navy, set the names in cream and keep the "&" cocoa only if it stays readable; otherwise make the "&" cream too.

## The motif: the loop

One continuous line around two dots. It always comes back to where it started.

- Navy line, cocoa dots. Never filled, never animated, never repeated as a pattern.
- **At most twice** on any one piece. Often once is better.
- It's the only motif. No florals, paisleys, lotuses, mandalas or borders.

## Space and layout

- Plenty of empty space. If in doubt, take something away.
- Thin stone-coloured lines to separate sections, not boxes or ornaments.
- Photos: our engagement photos, uncropped where possible, with a soft, warm, film-like finish. No filters, frames or tilted "polaroids".

## Words

- First person ("we", "us"), warm and plain. Short sentences.
- British spelling. No em dashes.
- Never: "embark", "journey", "cherish", "tie the knot", "tapestry", "fairytale".
- Don't explain ceremonies or traditions. Just name the events.

## Do and don't

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Navy text on cream | Gold, red, yellow, glitter |
| One cocoa italic word | Script fonts, all caps |
| Lots of white space | Florals, borders, patterns |
| The loop, used once | Hearts, rings, bows, Pinterest clichés |
| Our real photos | Stock images |
